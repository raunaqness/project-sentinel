# Settlement

Gateway Beta settles gross: the full captured amount is paid out and fees are invoiced monthly. Any settlement lower than the capture is therefore not a fee and must be raised with the acquirer.

# Refunds

Gateway Beta processes refunds in batches every 30 minutes.
