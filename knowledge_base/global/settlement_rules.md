# Timelines

Card and UPI payments settle T+1 business days after capture. Settlements are paid in a single daily batch per merchant.

# Net and gross settlement

Some gateways settle net of fees (fees deducted before payout) and others settle gross (full amount paid, fees invoiced separately). The gateway guide states which method applies. Under gross settlement, a settlement lower than the capture is never explained by fees.
