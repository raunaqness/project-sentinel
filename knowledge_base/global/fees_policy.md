# Policy

The merchant discount rate is set per merchant in the merchant's fee agreement and may differ by gateway. There is no platform-wide default rate.

A fee may only be used to explain a settlement difference when the merchant agreement documents both the rate and that it is deducted at settlement. Otherwise the difference must be investigated as an unexplained mismatch.
